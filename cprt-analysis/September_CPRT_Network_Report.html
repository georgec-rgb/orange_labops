September was a broadly good month. Five of seven labs improved as the dengue/flu season receded — BLR 1 (−0.070, best), NOA (−0.044), HYD (−0.059), NRL (−0.125). NRL’s flu surge unwound and its GM recovered 63% → 70%. The one exception is BLR 2 (+0.112) — but that is a reimbursement artifact (OCD reimbursement collapsed), not cost: its GM actually rose to 77%.

Adjusted CPRT jumped 1.103 → 1.215 (+0.112) — but this is a reimbursement artifact, not cost. Raw barely moved (1.206 → 1.219) and GM improved to 77.0%. The cause: OCD reimbursement collapsed ₹1.26L → ₹0.08L, so almost nothing is netted off. Flag for check (below).
⚠ BLR 2 — why did OCD reimbursement vanish (₹1.26L → ₹7.8k)? OCD reimbursement is earned on QC/calibration runs on the Ortho platform. A near-zero figure means either those runs moved off Ortho (to Alinity) or the reimbursement was not claimed/booked this month. The adjusted CPRT of 1.215 is almost entirely this missing credit — underlying cost is healthy (GM 77%). Check the OCD QC/calibration run booking for BLR 2.
