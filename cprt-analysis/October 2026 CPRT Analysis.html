Two different drivers, two different owners

CPRT effect Holding September's test mix, volume and ASP fixed, what did swapping in each test's actual October CPT (reagent/usage cost per test) cost or save? This isolates lab-side execution — wastage, repeats, QC/calibration drag — from everything else. Owned by the lab.
Test mix & ASP effect What did October's actual test mix (which tests got ordered, in what volume) and actual ASP (selling price realised) cost or save, versus the September-mix fixed base? This is a planning/demand signal, already priced into the AOP at most labs — not something a technician can fix on the bench. Owned by planning/commercial.

Network read-out

  • The network is on plan overall: actual 22.96% vs AOP 22.86% — about 11 bps on ₹1,513L of in-house revenue, roughly ₹1.6L for the month.
  • CPRT is running better than the fixed base network-wide, not worse: holding Sep mix/ASP/volume and swapping in actual Oct CPRT moves the network from 21.85% to 21.59% — ₹4.1L favourable, led by BLR 2 and HYD.
  • The gap to the fixed base is almost entirely test mix & ASP, not execution: network +1.37 pts, and it is nearly the whole story at MUM (+3.74 pts), GGN (+4.26 pts) and NOA (+4.46 pts). The AOP already prices this in for these labs.
  • BLR 1 is the only lab with a genuine CPRT drag (+₹1.39L) rather than a mix/ASP one.
  • BLR 2 remains the execution benchmark: 19.41% actual against a 20.56% fixed base.

Read before you act on these

  • MUM · HbA1c (Biorad) is the single worst test-level line in the whole network — both CPRT (+₹1.17L vs fixed base, ₹27.67→₹42.51 per test) and mix/volume are unfavourable together. This is the one test worth a direct conversation with the lab, not just a line in a table.
  • BLR 1 · Blood Group and Rh Typing shows a swing of −₹2.5L on a small-volume test — too large to be a clean reagent-usage story. Treat as a likely Sep-base CPT anomaly to sanity-check before presenting it as a saving.
  • BLR 1's “-Abbot” rows (Vitamin D, B12, TSH, FSH, LH, Progesterone, Ferritin, PSA, Prolactin, Estradiol, T4-Free) reflect the OCD→Alinity rename/migration, not new cost movement — their CPRT effect sits against a Sep-mix baseline that predates the switch. Same pattern as the Progesterone case already discussed; not a fresh finding.
  • HIV 1&2 Rapid shows a large mix-effect increase at nearly every lab (BLR1, BLR2, HYD, GGN, NOA) off a tiny September base (often under 110 tests). This is a genuine volume ramp-up, not noise — the % swing just looks dramatic against a small denominator.
