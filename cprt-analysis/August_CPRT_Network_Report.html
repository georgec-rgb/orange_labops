NRL's CPRT:CPT (ex-validation) jumped 1.24 → 1.35 (+0.11), well above its 1.20 target — but it is a seasonal respiratory surge, not structural. Consumption rose +41% (₹30.4L → ₹42.9L) on only +5% tests, because expensive molecular PCR panels exploded: Influenza Virus Panel +₹592k (57→230 tests, ~₹3,300/test) and H1N1 PCR +₹451k (31→319) — together ₹10.4L, or 83% of the ₹12.5L increase — plus Dengue ELISA +₹85k and COVID PCR +₹47k. That cost surge outran revenue, so GM fell 68.9% → 63.0% (−5.9pp). As the reference lab, NRL absorbs these esoteric molecular surges; they normalise once flu/monsoon season passes.
