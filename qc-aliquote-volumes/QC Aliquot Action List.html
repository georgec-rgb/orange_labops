Why these numbers are firmer than my earlier estimates Earlier I benchmarked each lab against the leanest lab. Subtracting QuidelOrtho’s aspiration volumes from what each lab draws showed the cushion plainly — MUM, GGN and NOA all sit at exactly 60 µL of overhead across three assays needing 80, 80 and 20 µL.
